Arkli Studios

A website showcasing a photographer's ability and talent.

Overview

Problem

The client approached me wanting to build him a portfolio website for his photography work. He wanted the website to be mainly focused on the images and its responsiveness.

Solution

After discussing what type of website he wanted, I started researching other photography portfolio websites and how they are structured. I showed some examples and what personally stood out to me to the client so we could agree on the approach.

Impact

Prior to this, the client didn't have a digital method to showcase his portfolio. With this, he has been able to attract clients more efficiently.

Process

01

Research

I conducted research by holding user interviews and surveys on multiple photography portfolios I picked out to gauge what users look for and like. Using methods such as Think Aloud testing made it more efficient in gathering qualitative data. With this data, I was able to understand what I need to do.

02

Ideation

I started doing rough sketches of multiple different types of structures and gave them to the client so they could select which one they preferred the most. After that, I started the wireframing, mainly focusing on the features and functions the client requested. I made sure to keep 100% transparency with the client, staying in constant communication for every design decision I made and any further ideas I thought of they might like.

03

Design

Once agreeing on the design and layout, I started to code it using HTML, CSS and JavaScript. I proposed ideas in terms of animation and transitions to the client which they fully agreed with.

04

Testing

Once finished, I ran the same tests I did when researching different photography portfolio websites. This allowed me to gauge exactly how it compares to other examples and what I've done uniquely, and also what I am missing.
